## Examples of Tensors
1. 1D Tensor: A scalar
2. 2D Tensor: A vector *of arbitrary size*
3. 3D Tensor: A matrix *of arbitrary size*
4. 4D Tensor: A **list** of *matrices*, e.g.,
	- An image may be represented as a 4D Tensor of size 3, where each matrix (of dimension $\text{width}\times\text{height}$) represents the RGB values of the image.
