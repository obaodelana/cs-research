#### Preprocessing
Before a dataset is used in a [[Neural Networks|neural network]], it is first *transformed* (pre-processed). In this notebook, we
- Crop the images to all be of the same size.
- Turn each image to a tensor (in this case, a 3D tensor, i.e., vector of 3 matrices).
- Normalize the tensors to all have a specified mean and standard deviation.
