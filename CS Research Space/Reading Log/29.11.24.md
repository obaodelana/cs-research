## Missing Data?
- In **clinical trials** some participants might drop out (or die) before the test ends, leading to missing measurements. This is particularly prevalent in long-term studies. Traditional approaches might discard or attempt imputation of the data of the patient, however, discarding decreases prediction accuracy, while imputing might introduce bias. The latter is especially the case for rare diseases, as there is very little data to make proper statistical inferences.
